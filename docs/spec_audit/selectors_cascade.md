Notes on the baseline:

- The January 2026 Selectors 4 draft differs from the 2022 draft that the Donner source comments
  cite. It removed `:target-within`, deferred `:local-link`, `:blank`, the time-dimensional
  pseudo-classes and the grid-structural (column) selectors to Level 5, and made the arguments of
  `:has()` and of `:nth-child(... of S)` unforgiving. The grammar production for combinators still
  lists `||`.
- CSS Scoping 1 has only its 2014 First Public Working Draft on /TR/. SVG 2 cites it for
  use-element shadow-tree encapsulation; most of the 2014 text (old `@scope`, `::shadow`,
  `/deep/`) is obsolete and is recorded as not applicable.
- Cascade 5 repeats Cascade 4 and adds layers; Cascade 6 repeats the cascade section and adds
  `@scope`. Rules common to several levels have one row under the lowest level that defines them.

## Inventory

Derivation: every row of the Selectors 4 overview table (section 2), plus every numbered section
of Selectors 4 sections 3 to 16 that defines a selector, a grammar rule or an error-handling rule;
the Selectors 3 conformance section; every numbered section of Cascade 4 sections 2 to 7; the
additions of Cascade 5 (sections 2, 4.8, 6.4, 7.3.5, 8) and Cascade 6 (sections 2.1, 2.5, 3); and
the CSS Scoping 1 sections that SVG 2 relies on.

Out of scope for an SVG user agent without script, and why: Selectors 4 section 17 (API hooks for
DOM methods; only the `:scope` fallback is covered), Appendix A (guidance for document-language
authors), Appendix C (`:visited` privacy), the CSSOM sections of Cascade 5 and 6, and the input,
resource-state and display-state pseudo-classes (HTML form, media and top-layer state that no SVG
element can have; each still has a row recording how Donner treats the name). CSS tokenization
and escaping, property-name case, `@media`, `@supports`, `@namespace` syntax and `@font-face` are
owned by other ledgers and appear here only where the cascade depends on them.
